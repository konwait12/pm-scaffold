# 001 Business Requirements

## Goal

Explain the business need before designing the system: why the change is needed, who participates, how the business event develops, and what outcome matters.

## Core Questions

- What triggered the requirement and what happens today?
- Which roles participate across the business lifecycle?
- What must each role achieve, and why?

## Entry

At least one registered source and an identifiable business fact owner. Sparse input may enter clarification but must not be treated as confirmed.

## Work Items

1. `project-background-goal`
2. `user-journey-and-stories`

## Required Outputs

Confirmed background and goals, lifecycle journey map, and story cards traceable to journey entries.

## Conditional Support

Business-mode competitive research and solution assessment when the business approach is unclear or has material alternatives.

## Do Not

Do not design screens, fields, APIs, architecture, or implementation tasks. Do not turn brainstormed scenarios into confirmed requirements.

## Human Owners And Exit

The business fact owner confirms background, goals, roles, journey, and stories. Open items have owners and known downstream impact.

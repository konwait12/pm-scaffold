# Product UX Source Handling

The confirmed journey/story artifact is the primary source. Background goals and constraints remain governing evidence.

- A feature requires at least one selected `ST-XXX` or an explicit human decision.
- Journey branches inform UX paths but do not automatically become separate features.
- Competitor evidence is reference material, not a product fact.
- Solution-assessment results affect the artifact only after the decision owner selects an option.
- New scope discovered during UX returns to the earliest affected upstream work item.

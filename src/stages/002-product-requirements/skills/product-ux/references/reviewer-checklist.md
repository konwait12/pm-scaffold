# Human Review Checklist: Product UX

- [ ] Does the scope satisfy the confirmed business need without hidden additions?
- [ ] Is the function list complete enough to explain what the product will contain?
- [ ] Can each P0 story be followed through a clear UX path?
- [ ] Are roles, systems, entry points, branches and states understandable?
- [ ] Are material alternatives and exclusions explicitly decided?
- [ ] Have detailed rules been left for function description?
- [ ] Can this baseline be handed to the function-description review?

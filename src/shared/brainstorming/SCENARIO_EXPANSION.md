# Scenario Expansion

Scenario expansion is triggered when a journey, UX flow, or function has a credible coverage gap. It is especially useful after a first journey draft, but is not mandatory at every work-item boundary.

Inspect lifecycle, roles, normal/alternate/exception/failure/timeout, permission, data condition, handoff, dependency, cancellation, retry, rollback and change-recovery dimensions. Present candidates with evidence and impact. Human disposition is one of `include`, `exclude`, `defer`, or `research`; unknown answers become ordinary QuestionRecords rather than a separate branch.
